Website Site Plan

Mundo Creativo Digital

1. Site Name & Logo

Site Name: Mundo Creativo Digital

Reasoning: This name represents a digital hub focused on providing resources, templates, and courses for creative digital design and web development.

2. Site Purpose

The site serves as a platform to showcase creative design assets, offer tutorials on web tools and design software, and provide contact options for custom digital design services.

3. Scenarios

  • Scenario 1: Where can I find downloadable templates and design assets for my next project?
  • Scenario 2: How can I contact the team to request custom graphic design or web development services?

4. Color Schema

The site uses the following palette to maintain a sleek, professional, and accessible look:

#0F4C81 Primary (Headings & Header)
#E05263 Accent (Buttons & Highlights)
#F4F7F6 Background (Light Gray/White)

5. Typography

The site uses Google Fonts for clear readability and hierarchy:

  • Montserrat (Sans-Serif): Used for all primary section headers (H1, H2, H3).
  • Open Sans (Sans-Serif): Used for body text, paragraph content, lists, and navigation links.
